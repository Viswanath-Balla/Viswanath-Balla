## ✦ A little about me

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering student specializing in **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at JNTU Hyderabad**. I like building things that connect intelligent models with useful, well-engineered software.

My interests span **machine learning, Generative AI, NLP, retrieval-augmented generation, and agentic systems**. I enjoy the full journey: understanding a problem, working with data, evaluating models, and turning the result into an application.
